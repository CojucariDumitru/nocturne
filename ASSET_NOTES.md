# Asset provenance

## Cinematic background

`assets/night-drive.png` is the original NOCTURNE background generated with the built-in image generation tool. It is atmospheric artwork, not a photo of a specific vehicle offered for sale.

Prompt summary: an anonymous black widebody 1990s Japanese sports coupe on wet asphalt beneath an industrial concrete highway at night, rear three-quarter view, front pointing left, red rear light, silver and pale green reflections, low camera, wide landscape frame, no people, words, logos or watermark.

## Catalogue photographs

Eight existing photographs of the corresponding car models were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons. Their full file titles, authors, download locations and license URLs are preserved in `assets/cars/credits.json`. The public `#/credits` page displays attribution. Raw photo bytes are unmodified. CSS uses cover crops; visitors can open the original aspect ratio in a lightbox.

Photos are model illustrations and may differ from the demo card's year, trim, market or equipment. Demo prices, mileage and stock statuses do not describe the photographed car.

## Drift Division redesign — 27 October 2026

**assets/drift/night-session.webp** is a new original generated hero asset, not a documentary photograph of a Formula DRIFT event, a named venue or a vehicle offered for sale. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool; retained in the site as a 1774×887 WebP (quality 87, format optimization only). Existing model photography and credits are unchanged.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic hero photograph for NOCTURNE MOTORS, a Japanese legends dealership with a new worldwide drift culture concept. Create a very wide landscape night motorsport photograph, approximately 2:1 aspect. A white Japanese Nissan Silvia S15 style drift coupe with restrained acid lime geometric accents and no sponsor logos is powersliding on a closed circuit at night. Close low front three-quarter camera, the whole car fits within the bottom/right two thirds of the frame, countersteering front wheels, substantial sculptural white tire smoke trailing toward the far right, black deep background and wet textured asphalt, realistic wheels and body proportions. Warm pale headlight beams and hard silver highlights, a little lime light reflecting on asphalt, restrained distant amber track floodlights. Top third and left quarter mostly dark negative space, very useful for a giant website headline. Premium gritty automotive editorial photography, direct flash, high dynamic range with deep blacks, subtle analog grain, panning motion only in surroundings, car remains tack sharp. Mood: controlled chaos, raw mechanical precision, underground Japanese drift culture. Do not add any typography, text, watermark, badges or real event logos. This is an original visual concept, not a real photograph of a competition or actual inventory. No crowds, no street racing, no city traffic.

**assets/drift/world-land.svg** uses land geometry from [Natural Earth, 1:110m](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson), projected to an equirectangular view and rendered as a dotted SVG. [Natural Earth data is public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Map coordinates show approximate venue areas and are not directions to an entrance. Region controls are UI markers rather than precise positions.

The tandem diagram is original code-native SVG artwork. Its curves are an illustrative movement study, not a real venue layout. Animated angles, car spacing and timing are graphic controls, not telemetry, a physics simulation or a scoring predictor.

NOCTURNE × FORMULA DRIFT is explicitly an unofficial creative collaboration concept. No sponsorship, event access, affiliation or licensing agreement is asserted. Organization names are references; no third-party official logo files or event photography were copied for this redesign.
